Privacy Policy

RetroLAB ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect and Purpose

Our application uses Google OAuth API to sign in. During this process, we access your Google account email address and profile name solely for user identification purposes.

In addition, to provide the calendar synchronization feature, we request Read and Write permissions for the Google Calendar API. This is strictly used to view (read) your Google Calendar events within the app and to sync (write) any events you add, modify, or delete back to your Google Calendar. We DO NOT store, collect, or transmit your personal info or Google Calendar data to any external backend servers.

This data may remain on your device after logging out for offline viewing and will be permanently deleted only when you manually clear your browser's cache and site data.
